About us

Welcome to SOAR Kafé!

Our journey to this exact moment has been one of growth, learning, and a deep love for serving our community. We didn't start out as an independent brand; our roots in the cafe world began by partnering with a well-known franchise, Chai Villa. Operating under a larger brand taught us the true art of hospitality. It's where we learned what it takes to build a welcoming space, serve the perfect cup of our classic 12-rupee special tea, and connect with our daily customers.

But as the years passed, our vision began to outgrow the franchise model. We wanted the freedom to experiment with our menu, redesign our environment, and build a brand that was entirely our own. Taking a leap of faith, we transformed our space and stepped into the world of independent cafe ownership. That leap gave birth to SOAR Kafé.

Today, located right in the vibrant heart of the college campuses on Gangapur Road, SOAR Kafé represents our fresh start. We have completely reimagined our identity, pouring our passion into every single detail, from curating an exciting new menu to redesigning our interiors with a calming dry sage green and cream pie white aesthetic.

We created SOAR Kafé to be more than just a place to grab a bite; we designed it to be your daily retreat. Whether you're taking a break between classes, catching up with friends, or finding a cozy corner to relax, our doors are open.

This is our new beginning, and we are so excited to share it with you. Welcome to the new standard. Welcome to SOAR Kafé.
